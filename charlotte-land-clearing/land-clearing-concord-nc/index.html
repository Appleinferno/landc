Local Know-How

Land Clearing Across Concord & Cabarrus County

Wooded Residential Lots Near Poplar Tent, Odell School & NC-73

On the west side of Concord—around Poplar Tent Road, George Liles Parkway, Weddington Road, and Odell School Road—remaining residential lots are often heavily wooded with mature oaks, poplars, and pines. We mark the house footprint, septic or utility corridors, and driveway with you before a machine unloads, clearing what the builder needs and saving the shade trees that buffer you from the road and neighbors.

Acreage & Homesteads in Eastern Cabarrus County

East of US-29 and NC-49 toward Flowes Store Road, Miami Church Road, Gold Hill Road, and Mount Pleasant, parcels stretch into rolling five- to thirty-acre tracts. Here the work often combines full land clearing for the home site, detached shop, and driveway with selective clearing or forestry mulching for pasture and sightlines. Phasing the clearing lets you open the building area now and expand pastures later.

Infill & Small Commercial Parcels Along US-29 & Church Street

In-town lots and commercial sites along Concord Parkway (US-29), Church Street, and Branchview Drive require tight access planning, utility awareness, and coordination with general contractors. We clear to the staked limits of disturbance, keep streets clean, and stay on schedule so grading and utility crews aren't held up.

Rocky River, Coddle Creek & Cabarrus Soils

Concord drains through Coddle Creek, Cold Water Creek, Irish Buffalo Creek, and Clarke Creek into the Rocky River, with water-supply watershed protections around Coddle Creek Reservoir. Once vegetation comes off Cabarrus clay and slate-belt slopes, exposed soil can wash fast in heavy rain. We keep equipment out of regulated stream buffers, avoid rutting wet clay, and leave mulch stabilization where appropriate.

City of Concord Rules, Erosion Permits & HOAs

Inside city limits, the City of Concord Development Ordinance regulates tree preservation, perimeter buffers, and commercial or subdivision site work, and North Carolina requires an approved erosion and sedimentation control plan for disturbances of one acre or more. Always confirm permitting, buffer, and HOA requirements with the City of Concord or Cabarrus County before clearing begins.

Questions

Concord Land Clearing FAQs

Do I need a permit to clear a lot in Concord, NC?

It depends on the parcel location, zoning, and area disturbed. Disturbing one acre or more generally requires a state/local erosion control plan, and the City of Concord Development Ordinance has tree-save and buffer rules for development sites and stream corridors. Confirm what applies to your lot with the City of Concord or Cabarrus County.

Do you remove stumps when clearing for a house or shop?

Yes. Inside house footprints, driveways, and outbuilding sites, we offer full stump removal as well as stump grinding so your grading crew has clean ground.

Do you grade the house pad after clearing the trees?

No. We do not perform grading or dirt work. We clear the trees, brush, and stumps and hand off a clean envelope to your builder or grading contractor.

Can you clear just part of a multi-acre tract in Cabarrus County?

Yes. Most rural acreage owners clear the driveway and home site completely while leaving perimeter woods intact or mulching just the underbrush around the yard.

Can you work near Coddle Creek or Cold Water Creek?

Yes, outside of protected stream buffers. We'll identify any creeks or wet swales during the walk-through and recommend confirming buffer widths with the city or county.

How long does it take to clear a residential lot in Concord?

Most single-family building envelopes take one to three days on site depending on tree density, diameter, and stump handling.

Can you provide a Certificate of Insurance for my builder or HOA?

Yes. We are fully insured and send certificates directly to your general contractor, lender, or HOA upon request.

How much does land clearing cost in Concord?

Cost depends on the size of the area, tree size and density, stump removal needs, slope, and access. We provide a firm written price after a free on-site walk-through.
