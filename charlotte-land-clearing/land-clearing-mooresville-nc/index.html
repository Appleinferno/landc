Local Know-How

Clearing Lake Lots, Infill & Acreage in Mooresville

Lake Norman Peninsula & Waterfront Lots Off Brawley School Road & Perth Road

Lots on the Brawley School Road peninsula, off Perth Road, and around Morrison Plantation and Langtree Road are often sloped, tight between neighboring homes, and governed by strict shoreline rules. Clearing a lake lot is rarely about taking every tree down; it's about removing the trees and stumps inside the house and pool footprint while preserving mature oaks and pines along the bank and opening framed sightlines to the water.

Lake Norman Shoreline Buffers & Duke Energy Rules

Waterfront and cove lots on Lake Norman fall under overlapping rules: Iredell County and Town of Mooresville watershed/riparian buffers (commonly a 50-foot undisturbed buffer measured from normal full pond) plus Duke Energy Shoreline Management Guidelines near the 760-foot full-pond contour. Before we start a machine on any lakefront parcel, we walk the buffer line with you and recommend confirming your approved site plan with the Town of Mooresville, Iredell County, and Duke Energy Lake Services.

Acreage East of I-77 Along NC-150, NC-115 & Shearers Road

Head east of I-77 and downtown Mooresville toward Shearers Road, Mazeppa Road, Patterson Farm Road, and Coddle Creek Highway (NC-3), and the terrain opens into rolling southern Iredell County acreage. Here we clear custom home sites, long private driveways, detached workshops, and horse pastures, often phasing the work so the building envelope is cleared for construction first.

Slopes, Red Clay & Low Ground-Pressure Equipment

Lake-area lots and creek draws draining into Dye Creek, Reed Creek, and Back Creek drop quickly toward the water over Piedmont red clay. Running heavy wheeled skidders on sloped clay invites washouts into the cove. We use low ground-pressure tracked equipment, time work around wet weather, and leave mulch cover outside the structural footprint to hold the soil until your grader sets silt fence.

Town of Mooresville UDO, HOA Review & Hand-Off

The Town of Mooresville Unified Development Ordinance includes tree preservation and buffer standards for development, and lake communities such as The Point, Waterside, and Harbor Cove require architectural review before trees come down. We flag clearing limits and keeper trees on the ground so you can document them for your HOA, and we hand off a stump-free building envelope ready for your grading contractor.

Questions

Mooresville Land Clearing FAQs

Can you clear a waterfront lot on Lake Norman in Mooresville?

Yes. We clear the building envelope, driveway, and pool area outside the protected shoreline buffer while keeping heavy equipment out of the 50-foot buffer and below the 760-foot contour. Always confirm buffer and view-corridor rules with Iredell County, the Town of Mooresville, and Duke Energy.

Do I need a permit to clear land in Mooresville, NC?

Requirements depend on whether the parcel is inside Town of Mooresville limits or unincorporated Iredell County, watershed rules, and total area disturbed (one acre or more generally requires an erosion control plan). Confirm with the town or county planning office before work starts.

My Lake Norman HOA requires tree removal approval. Can you help?

Yes. During our walk-through we flag the building envelope limits and the keeper trees so you can photograph them or walk the lot with your HOA architectural reviewer.

Do you pull stumps inside the house and pool footprint?

Yes. Full stump removal and stump grinding are offered so your builder or pool contractor doesn't have decaying wood under foundations, slabs, or driveways.

Do you do the finish grading or build house pads?

No. We handle the land clearing, brush mulching, and stump removal, leaving the footprint clean and ready for your grading contractor.

Can you clear acreage on the east side of Mooresville off NC-150 or NC-3?

Yes. We regularly clear multi-acre tracts for custom homes, barns, driveways, and pasture across southern Iredell County.

What happens to the cleared trees and brush?

Underbrush and smaller wood are mulched in place where erosion cover is helpful, and logs or root balls from the house footprint are handled according to your written scope.

How much does land clearing cost in Mooresville?

Price depends on lot size, slope, tree count and diameter, access, and stump removal requirements. We provide a firm written price after walking the lot with you.
