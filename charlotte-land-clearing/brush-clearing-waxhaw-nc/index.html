Local Know-How

Brush Clearing Tailored to Waxhaw & Union County Properties

Horse Farms, Board Fences & Pasture Edges Along New Town & Cuthbertson Road

Western Union County is known for horse properties and multi-acre homesteads along New Town Road, Cuthbertson Road, Billy Howey Road, and Marvin Road. Over a few summers, Chinese privet, wild cherry, honeysuckle, and blackberry canes creep out of the woodline and swallow board fences and woven wire. Because wilted wild cherry leaves are a livestock hazard when limbs drop into a paddock and thick brush hides broken rails, Land Clearing Charlotte pushes the woodline back with a tracked mulcher, shreds the brush outside the pasture, and hand-clears right against fence posts and gates.

Brush Clearing Cost in Waxhaw ($2,000–$8,000 per Acre) & Method Comparison

Brush clearing in Waxhaw typically runs $2,000 to $8,000 per acre depending on property conditions, while stump grinding and full stump removal are quoted separately. Four site factors determine where your project falls in that range: (1) thick brush density, (2) the amount of tree removal involving trees thicker than 8 inches, (3) steep banks or hilly terrain, and (4) rocks that equipment must navigate around. Unlike a tractor bush hog that leaves jagged sapling stobs that can puncture tractor tires or injure horses, our rotary drum mulcher grinds stems flush with the ground—and where larger deadfall, tree removal over 8 inches, or haul-away is needed near formal entryways, we scope those items clearly in your written estimate.

Wooded Estate Lots, Acreage & Pre-Work Fence / Utility Checklist

Many homes around Providence Road South (NC-16), Kensington Road, Hunter Oaks, MillBridge, and Lawson—as well as larger tracts south of NC-75 toward Cane Creek Park, Jaars Road, and Brady Road—have rear woods, farm lanes, or ditches choked with greenbrier and sweetgum. Before unloading equipment, we walk the area with you to flag survey pins, hidden field wire, T-posts, well heads, and septic lines, and we recommend a free NC 811 locate before any below-grade stump work.

Twelve Mile Creek, Cane Creek & Town of Waxhaw / Union County Rules

Waxhaw sits across the headwaters of Twelve Mile Creek, Waxhaw Creek, and the Cane Creek water-supply watershed draining toward the Catawba River. The Town of Waxhaw Unified Development Ordinance (UDO) and Union County Development Regulation enforce tree preservation, heritage/specimen tree protections on qualifying parcels, and undisturbed perennial and intermittent stream buffers, while the North Carolina Sedimentation Pollution Control Act of 1973 (G.S. 113A-57) requires an approved erosion control plan before disturbing one acre or more of soil. During our walk-through we identify any creek banks or wet swales and advise confirming buffer rules with the Town of Waxhaw, Union County, or your HOA before clearing near water.

HOA Ponds & 100% Mechanical Mulch-In-Place Finish

Subdivisions around Waxhaw rely on retention and detention ponds that grow up fast in willow and sweetgum saplings. We work with HOA boards to clear pond banks, dam slopes, and access easements mechanically—using hand tools around riprap and concrete outlet risers with zero chemical sprays—and provide a board-ready written scope and Certificate of Insurance.

Waxhaw Brush Clearing FAQs

Can you clear brush along a wood or wire horse fence in Waxhaw without damaging it?

Yes. We run the tracked mulcher parallel to the fence to open the corridor and switch to hand saws and brush cutters for stems growing right against boards, posts, gates, or wire.

Do I need a permit from the Town of Waxhaw or Union County to clear brush?

Cutting back overgrown brush, briars, and small saplings on an existing residential lot or pasture edge generally does not require a permit because roots and soil remain in place. However, the Town of Waxhaw Unified Development Ordinance (UDO) tree-save and specimen tree rules, HOA conservation buffers, Twelve Mile Creek and Cane Creek stream buffers, and soil disturbances of one acre or more under NC G.S. 113A-57 carry specific requirements. Confirm with the Town of Waxhaw, Union County, or your HOA if you aren't sure.

What's the difference between brush clearing, bush-hogging, and forestry mulching?

Tractor bush-hogging is suited for open pasture grass and light weeds, whereas our tracked rotary drum mulcher grinds woody saplings, thick briars, and fence-line overgrowth flush to the ground. Brush clearing targets overgrown lots, briar patches, fence lines, ditches, and pond banks, while forestry mulching focuses on selective understory clearing beneath a forest canopy, trails, and pasture reclamation.

Can you clear wild cherry and privet from pasture edges, and what about larger trees?

Yes. We cut and mulch encroaching saplings and privet along paddock perimeters and push the brush line back so limbs aren't hanging over the fence. If your project involves removing trees thicker than 8 inches or grinding/removing stumps below grade, we scope those items in writing and quote stump work separately.

Do you use chemical sprays to kill briars and vines?

No. Everything we do is 100% mechanical cutting and mulching. We never apply herbicides or chemicals, especially around pastures, horses, wells, and creeks.

Can you clear an overgrown retention pond for a Waxhaw HOA?

Yes. We clear woody saplings and brush from pond banks, dam embankments, riprap, and access easements for HOAs, with a board-ready written scope and Certificate of Insurance.

What if there is old farm wire hidden in the brush?

We walk the area first to flag old field wire, T-posts, survey monuments, and utility boxes so we can pull wire clear or stop the mulching head safely short.

How much does brush clearing cost in Waxhaw?

Brush clearing in Waxhaw typically runs $2,000 to $8,000 per acre depending on four factors: thick brush density, the amount of tree removal involving trees thicker than 8 inches, steep banks or hilly terrain, and rocks that equipment must navigate around. Stump grinding and full stump removal are quoted separately. We give a firm written price after a free on-site walk-through.
