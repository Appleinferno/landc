Our Commitment to Your Privacy: We Do Not Sell Your Information

Land Clearing Charlotte ("we," "our," or "us") operates as a professional land clearing and forestry mulching service in North Carolina. We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information or contact details to third parties or data brokers. Personal information collected through our website is solely used to evaluate your property, communicate estimates, schedule on-site walkthroughs, and deliver land clearing services.

1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Land Clearing Charlotte collects, uses, protects, and discloses information gathered when you visit our website (including landclearingcharlotte.com and its subdirectories such as /lot-clearing-tips/), submit our free estimate forms, or interact with our online services.

By using our website, submitting an inquiry, or requesting an estimate, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

As a lead generation and service fulfillment website, we collect information in two primary ways:

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

When you fill out our "Request an Estimate" form, call our office, or email us, you provide:

  • Contact Details: Full name, telephone number, and email address.
  • Property & Project Information: Property address, city, county, acreage size, vegetation description, and specific clearing objectives.
  • Communications: Any notes, voicemails, or photos submitted for site assessment.

B. Automatically Collected Technical Data

When you navigate our website, our servers and third-party analytics platforms automatically record technical data including your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, pages viewed, time spent per page, and interaction clicks.

3. Third-Party Analytics & Advertising Tracking Technologies

To measure marketing effectiveness, improve user experience, and connect with property owners across Mecklenburg and neighboring counties, our website deploys tracking pixels and analytics tags from trusted advertising and analytics partners:

Tracking Provider Technology Used Purpose & Data Collected
Google Analytics Analytics Cookies & Tag Manager Measures website traffic, popular blog guides, bounce rates, and device usage trends. Data is aggregated and anonymized.
Google Ads Conversion Tracking & Remarketing Tags Measures ad campaign performance, tracks quote form completions from Google searches, and displays relevant service reminders on the Google Display Network.

These third-party platforms use cookies, web beacons, and unique identifiers. For details on how cookies function and instructions on how to manage or disable them, please consult our dedicated Cookies Policy.

4. How We Use Your Information

We process personal information exclusively for legitimate business purposes associated with lead generation and contract execution:

  • Preparing accurate, competitive proposals for forestry mulching, land clearing, and site prep.
  • Contacting you via telephone, email, or SMS to schedule on-site walkthroughs.
  • Answering questions regarding machinery, tree preservation, and municipal permitting.
  • Monitoring and optimizing our website speed, navigation, and mobile usability.
  • Evaluating the return on our digital advertising investments across Google, Meta, and Pinterest.
  • Fulfilling legal obligations, verifying property boundary authorizations, and preventing fraudulent requests.

5. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We disclose information strictly under the following limited circumstances:

  • Authorized Service Providers: Trusted technical vendors who assist in operating our website, web hosting infrastructure, CRM lead storage, and transactional email delivery. These vendors are contractually bound to maintain confidentiality and may not use your data for any other purpose.
  • Legal Compliance: When required by federal or state law, court order, subpoena, or to protect the safety, rights, and property of Land Clearing Charlotte, our clients, or the public.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of company assets, client records and lead communications may be transferred as an operational business asset.

6. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards (including SSL/TLS 256-bit encryption for data in transit) to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure.

We retain project inquiries and contact information for as long as necessary to fulfill project quotes, maintain warranty records, and comply with state accounting and legal standards.

7. Your Privacy Rights & Choices

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you have specific rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right of Access & Portability: Request confirmation of whether we hold personal information about you and receive a copy of that data.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate contact or property details.
  • Right to Deletion: Request that we delete your contact records from our active lead database (subject to legal or contractual retention obligations).
  • Marketing Opt-Out: Opt out of future promotional emails or text messages at any time by replying "STOP" or emailing our privacy contact.

8. Children's Online Privacy

Our website is directed exclusively toward property owners, builders, and adults seeking commercial land management services. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 18.
