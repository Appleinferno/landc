Understanding Your Estimate

What Turns a Planning Range Into a Firm Written Quote

Our calculator models how brush density, slope, rock, and tree diameter affect machine production per acre across the Carolina Piedmont. When we walk your property in person, we confirm four additional details to give you an exact price:

  1. 01

    Exact Work Boundaries

    You rarely need to clear every square foot on your deed. Flagging the specific homesite envelope, driveway corridor, or pasture edge keeps your cost focused on the ground you will actually use.

  2. 02

    Mulching vs. Stump Removal

    High-speed forestry mulching processes brush and saplings in place and leaves a protective mulch layer. Structural building footprints or driveways that require full stump and root excavation are scoped separately.

  3. 03

    Keeper Tree Preservation

    Working selectively around mature oaks, hickories, and boundary buffers requires precision machine control so root zones and bark stay undamaged while the understory is opened up.

  4. 04

    Access & Mobilization

    Gate width, culvert capacity, wet red-clay low spots, and proximity to fences or utilities determine which tracked carrier and attachments we dispatch to your parcel.
