Brush Clearing Cost in Concord ($2,000–$8,000 per Acre) & Method Comparison

Brush clearing in Concord typically runs $2,000 to $8,000 per acre depending on property conditions, while stump grinding and full stump removal are quoted separately. Four site factors determine where your project lands in that range: (1) thick brush density, (2) the amount of tree removal involving trees thicker than 8 inches, (3) steep banks or hilly terrain, and (4) rocks that equipment must navigate around (including Carolina Slate Belt fieldstone in eastern Cabarrus County). While a tractor bush hog can handle open pasture grass, our tracked rotary drum mulcher grinds dense briars and saplings flush to the ground in one pass—and right next to fences, gates, utility boxes, and culverts, we switch to hand saws and trimmers so structures stay safe.

Concord Brush Clearing FAQs

I got an overgrowth notice from the City of Concord. Can you help?

Tell us the address and the deadline on your notice when you call, and we'll let you know right away if we can schedule it in time. While routine above-ground brush mulching generally does not require a grading permit, the City of Concord Development Ordinance (CDO Article 11) tree-save and stream buffer rules and NC G.S. 113A-57 (for soil disturbance of one acre or more) still apply—always confirm specific compliance questions with the City of Concord.

Can you clear a corridor for a new fence installation in Concord?

Yes. We clear a clean three- to ten-foot working strip along your marked boundary line so your fence crew can pull string and set posts without fighting privet and briars.

Can you clear saplings from our HOA retention pond embankment?

Yes. We cut and mulch woody growth on pond banks, dam slopes, and access paths, cutting stems flush and using hand tools around riprap and concrete risers so compacted dam soil stays undisturbed. We provide a written proposal and Certificate of Insurance for your board.

Do you regrade ditches when you clear them, or remove larger trees and stumps?

We clear the brush, vines, and saplings from ditches and culvert openings so water can move and you can inspect the channel, but we do not excavate or regrade soil. If your job also requires removing trees thicker than 8 inches or grinding/removing stumps, we scope those items in writing and quote stump work separately.

Do you spray weed killer or brush chemicals?

No. Everything we do is 100% mechanical cutting and mulching.

Do I need to be on site if I live out of town?

No. We can confirm the scope by phone, walk the Concord property ourselves, and send you before-and-after photos as soon as the cleanup is finished.

What if there is trash or old wire fence hidden in the brush?

We walk the lot before unloading the mulcher to flag tires, wire fencing, concrete, survey pins, or utility boxes so nothing dangerous is hit and we can plan around it in the scope.

How much does brush clearing cost in Concord?

Brush clearing in Concord typically runs $2,000 to $8,000 per acre depending on four factors: thick brush density, the amount of tree removal involving trees thicker than 8 inches, steep banks or hilly terrain, and rocks that equipment must navigate around. Stump grinding and full stump removal are quoted separately. We give a firm written price after a free on-site walk-through.
