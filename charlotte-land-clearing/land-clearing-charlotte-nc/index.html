How It Works

What Our Land Clearing Process Is

Every Charlotte clearing job follows the same four steps, so you know exactly what is happening on your property and what it costs before a machine ever shows up.

  1. 01

    Property Walk-Through

    We walk the lines with you, flag the trees you want to keep, and check access, slopes, drainage, utilities, and easements.

  2. 02

    Written Scope & Price

    You get a firm proposal: what gets cleared, to what finish, what stays, and when. No fuel surcharges or surprise add-ons.

  3. 03

    Clearing Day

    We bring the right tracked machine for your ground and clear to the agreed limits, protecting lawns, driveways, and root zones.

  4. 04

    Final Walk & Hand-Off

    We walk the finished site with you and confirm it's ready for your builder, grader, fence crew, or next phase.

Most residential lots in Charlotte go from walk-through to finished clearing in one to two weeks, depending on the season, the weather, and whether a permit is involved. Larger acreage and commercial jobs are phased so your builder or site contractor can follow right behind us instead of waiting on a single finish date.

Land Clearing, Done Right

Land Clearing Built Around Charlotte Properties

Residential Lot Clearing

Most of our Charlotte work is clearing single-family lots for a new home, an addition, or a backyard that has been swallowed by woods. Infill lots inside the city are often tight, with neighbors close by, overhead lines, and mature hardwoods worth saving. We plan equipment access before we start, clear to the footprint your builder needs, and leave the trees you want to keep standing and undamaged.

Acreage & Rural Tract Clearing

On larger tracts toward the edges of the county, the job is less about precision and more about production and planning. We clear in phases, opening up home sites, driveways, pasture, or building envelopes first and leaving buffers where they help with privacy, wind, or runoff. You decide how open the property ends up, and we price the job by the area and the density of what's growing on it.

Commercial & Development Site Clearing

For builders, developers, and general contractors, we clear to the limits on your plans and coordinate with your grading and erosion control contractors. We show up when scheduled, keep the site clean, and work around inspections, so the next trade can roll in right behind us.

Selective Clearing That Saves Your Best Trees

Clearing doesn't have to mean clear-cutting. Many Charlotte owners want the underbrush, dead timber, and scrub trees gone while keeping the oaks, hickories, and poplars that give a property its value and shade. We flag keepers with you during the walk-through and protect their root zones during clearing, because heavy traffic over roots can kill a tree years later.

Charlotte Soils, Slopes & Erosion

Charlotte sits on Piedmont red clay. Once the vegetation is gone, that clay sheds water fast and washes into streets, storm drains, and creeks. We use tracked machines with low ground pressure, avoid working soaked ground, and where it makes sense we leave a mulch layer or vegetated buffer to hold the soil until your next phase starts.

Permits & Rules to Know Before You Clear

North Carolina requires an approved erosion and sedimentation control plan for most land-disturbing projects over one acre, and Charlotte and Mecklenburg County enforce it locally. Charlotte's tree ordinance mainly applies to commercial and new development projects, and trees in the public right-of-way belong to the city. Creeks are protected by stream buffer rules. We'll point out anything on your property that may need a permit or approval before clearing begins, but always confirm the requirements with the city or county for your specific parcel.

What Affects the Cost of Land Clearing in Charlotte

  • Size of the area: square footage or acreage being cleared.
  • Vegetation density: light brush and saplings versus mature, dense woods.
  • Tree size: larger trees take more time and handling.
  • Finish level: mulched in place, cleared to grade, or stumps removed.
  • Access and slope: tight infill lots and steep ground slow production.
  • Keeper trees and buffers: selective work is more careful work.

Because every property is different, we price after we've walked it. That's how you get a number you can hold us to.

Questions

Land Clearing Charlotte FAQs

How much does land clearing cost in Charlotte, NC?

It depends on the size of the area, how dense the vegetation is, the size of the trees, access, slope, and the finish you need. A lightly wooded backyard costs far less per square foot than dense hardwoods on a slope. We give a firm written price after a free on-site walk-through.

Do I need a permit to clear land in Charlotte?

Projects that disturb more than one acre generally need an approved erosion and sedimentation control plan. Commercial and new development projects may also fall under Charlotte's tree ordinance, and work near creeks is limited by stream buffer rules. We'll flag what we see on your property, but confirm the requirements for your parcel with the city or county.

Can you clear my lot but keep certain trees?

Yes. Selective clearing is one of the most common requests we get. We flag the trees you want to keep during the walk-through and keep heavy equipment off their root zones while we clear around them.

What happens to the trees and brush after you clear?

On many jobs we mulch the material in place, which avoids haul-off costs and protects the soil. Where a clean, bare site is required for construction, we discuss handling and removal options as part of your written scope.

How long does it take to clear a residential lot?

Most typical residential lots are cleared in one to three working days once we're on site. Scheduling usually takes one to two weeks from approval, depending on the season and weather.

Will your equipment damage my yard or driveway?

We use tracked machines with low ground pressure, plan our access route in advance, and avoid working saturated ground. If there's a soft spot or a driveway to protect, we'll plan around it before we start.

Do you work with home builders and developers?

Yes. We regularly clear to the limits shown on site plans and coordinate with grading and erosion control contractors so the job moves straight into the next phase.

Is land clearing the same as forestry mulching?

Not exactly. Forestry mulching is one method of clearing that grinds vegetation into mulch on the spot. Land clearing is the broader job of getting a property ready for a specific use, and it may combine mulching, tree removal, and other methods depending on the goal.

Can you clear land in the winter?

Yes, and late fall through winter is often a great time to clear in Charlotte. With the leaves down, we can see the terrain and the trees better, and growth is dormant. We do pause after heavy rain to protect the soil.

What parts of Charlotte do you serve?

All of Charlotte and Mecklenburg County, including Steele Creek, Ballantyne, South Charlotte, University City, Mountain Island, and East Charlotte, plus surrounding towns like Huntersville and Mint Hill.
