Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Land Clearing

How much does it cost to clear an acre in the Greater Charlotte area?
Our typical per-acre prices range from $2,000 to $8,000 per acre depending on undergrowth density, the number of trees over 8 inches in diameter, steep slopes, and terrain obstacles. Stump grinding and full root-ball extraction are quoted separately after an on-site walkthrough, or you can preview a ballpark budget with our Land Clearing Cost Calculator.
What should I compare when getting quotes from land clearing companies?
Verify whether the operator who walks your property is the same person running the machine, confirm commercial liability insurance, and ask whether woody debris is mulched on site or hauled away. A reputable operator flags keeper trees and boundary stakes with you before any equipment unloads.
Do I need a permit before clearing my lot or acreage?
Simple underbrush mulching that leaves roots and topsoil undisturbed rarely requires a land-disturbance permit, whereas full root grubbing on larger parcels or work near stream buffers and tree-save zones may require county or municipal approval. We review boundaries and protected buffers with you during your free walkthrough.
